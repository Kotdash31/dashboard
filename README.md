
## 📊 Project Overview: KPI Dashboard for Product Category Performance

In this project, an interactive dashboard was created to visualize key KPI metrics and the growth dynamics of a product category.

## 📌 [Dashboard ](https://datalens.yandex/j425je9676zg4)

### 🎯 Dashboard Requirements

The dashboard is designed to help analyze data and answer the following key business questions:

- 📈 What is the revenue growth compared to the previous month?  
  *(Target: at least +5% MoM)*  
- 📦 What is the purchase (buyout) rate?  
  *(Target: 95%)*  
- 🏷️ What is the revenue growth by category and by brand?  
- 📊 What are the main trends and patterns observed?  
- 🔄 Is there any relationship between the buyout rate and product price?

---

### 🗃️ Dataset Description

- **Table 1 (CSV):** Sales data by month and brand, including revenue and buyout rates.
- **Table 2 (CSV):** SKU-level data, including SKU ID, subcategory, brand, price (for the last two months — November and December), revenue, average buyout rate, and country of origin.

---

### 🛠️ Dashboard Development Process

1. **Requirement gathering & dashboard canvas design**  
   - Defined who will use the dashboard, in what context, what problems it should help solve, and what questions it should answer.  
   - Identified available data and its structure.  
   - Selected appropriate visualization types.  
     [Have a look at canvas](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/2/folders/1mbSFYoaro2YRrq_SK0mgQIX-T2jovXDJ)

2. **Data preparation**  
   - Cleaned raw data from nulls and irrelevant values.  
   - Converted data types, performed aggregation and filtering.  
   - Created calculated metrics as needed.
   - Created final datasets

3. **Visualization building**  
   - Designed charts and visual elements based on the questions and KPIs.

4. **Dashboard layout & assembly**  
   - Built and styled the dashboard in Yandex DataLens.

---

📌 The result is a dynamic and user-friendly dashboard that enables business stakeholders to quickly assess performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.
